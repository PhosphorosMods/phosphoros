## Exactly what this covers

Paths are inside the mod jar (and under `src/main/resources/` in the source tree).

1. **Textures**: every file under `assets/lucifertotem/textures/` (`blocks/`, `entity/`, `environment/`, `gui/`,
   `items/`, `misc/`, `models/`, `painting/`), including `.png.mcmeta` animation files, **except** the files listed
   under "Not covered" below.
2. **Models**: every file under `assets/lucifertotem/models/` (block and item models) and
   `assets/lucifertotem/blockstates/`.
3. **Art**: the Lady Babylon paintings (`assets/lucifertotem/textures/painting/`), mob skins, the project icon
   and the promotional preview cards published with the mod. Screenshots or cards may show vanilla Minecraft
   blocks and items; those parts remain Mojang's.
4. **Music**: `assets/lucifertotem/sounds/records/devils_flight.ogg`, an original chiptune composed and synthesized
   for this mod (no samples).
